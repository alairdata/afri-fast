Questions? Answers.

Which foods does Logga know?

Logga is built around African meals: waakye, jollof, banku, kenkey, fufu, egusi, eba, ugali, kelewele and many more. Because you can snap a photo or describe your meal in your own words, it isn't limited to a fixed list. It works for everyday foods from anywhere too.

Is Logga free?

Yes. You can log 2 meals a day for free, along with your calorie target, weight trend, water, steps and insights. Logga Pro unlocks unlimited logging, all recipes, recipes built from your kitchen and deeper insights for $4.99 a month or $41.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.

Do I need to weigh my food?

No. Logga estimates portions from your photo or description, using everyday measures like a ball, a ladle, a cup or a plate. If you do weigh your food, type the grams and Logga uses them.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Logga is on the App Store for iPhone. On Android, you can use Logga in any phone or computer browser at logga.space/app.

How accurate are the calories?

Logga estimates calories from what's on your plate and how it's usually prepared, and lets you adjust any food or portion. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not lab measurements. Logga is a tracking tool, not medical advice.

What happens to my data?

Your logs are stored securely and used to run the app for you. You can delete your account and data at any time from Settings. Read the full privacy policy.
