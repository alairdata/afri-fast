Privacy Policy

Logga · Last updated: 30 September 2026

Logga ("we", "us") is a food, weight, hydration and activity tracking app available at logga.space. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, provided when you sign in with Google or create an account.
  • Data you log: meals and calories, weight, water intake, steps, activities, fasting sessions, check-ins, goals and profile settings (such as age, height and activity level).
  • Technical data: basic device and usage information needed to keep the app working and secure.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: saving your logs, showing your progress and syncing across your devices.
  • To generate insights and meal suggestions, using AI services (see below).
  • To send notifications you turn on, such as reminders and milestones.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them only to create and identify your account. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or other Google data.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run Logga. Your data is stored with Supabase and the app is hosted on Vercel. When you use AI features, the relevant text you submit (for example a meal description or a summary of your recent logs) is sent to AI providers such as Anthropic and Google to produce a response. We do not sell your personal data.

Health information

Logga is a tracking tool, not a medical service. It does not provide medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete individual entries in the app.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app's settings, or by contacting us below.
  • You can turn off notifications at any time.

Data retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account. We use industry-standard safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Children

Logga is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the date at the top.
