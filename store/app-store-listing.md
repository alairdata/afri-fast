## Description
Logga is a food and calorie tracker built for how Africans actually eat.

Most tracking apps don't know jollof rice, egusi, eba or suya. Logga does. Point your camera at your plate, or type what you ate, and get a calorie and nutrition estimate you can adjust in a tap.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Snap a meal and get calories and macros, including African dishes
• Set a daily calorie goal based on your body and your target weight
• Track water with one-tap quick add
• Log steps and workouts, and see your active days at a glance
• Watch your weight trend over weeks and months
• Build streaks for logging and hydration and celebrate milestones
• Get gentle insights on your patterns, not lectures
• Calendar view of every day you logged

BUILT TO BE CALM
No shaming, no red warnings for a bad day. Logga shows you the honest picture and helps you keep going.

YOUR DATA
Your logs are yours. You can delete your account and all your data from Settings at any time.

Logga is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise.
